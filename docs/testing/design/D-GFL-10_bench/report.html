Changing the gains online

Computed before the library made the change bumpless (0caa792): here ∫e was kept, so the integral term jumped by the ratio of the new to the old Kp/Ti at the change; the Kp 5 / Ti 5 ms trip comes from that jump. The model now carries the integral term over, as the firmware does; rerun the sweep to refresh this table. On the bench the gains change while connected (keys + − > <; the telemetry reports kp and ti; every capture and firmware dump records the gains in force). The model applies the same change: today's gains (Kp 3.5 V/A, Ti 20 ms) through the connection, the new gains 0.3 s after the contact, the 0.1 A step at 0.6 s. run(gains=...) takes such a schedule, and bench_gain_schedule() rebuilds it from a bench console (the key presses since the last boot).

Limits

  • A result here never passes or fails the firmware (ADR 0004).
