# D-GFL-10: current PI gains (Kp, Ti) on the fitted step 2b model

Design test ([ADR 0004](../../../adr/0004-averaged-model-for-fast-evaluation.md)), run at Luiz Villa's request on 2026-11-13: averaged model, **INFO only, no verdict**. It gives a ballpark of the current PI gains worth trying on the bench with the keys + / − (Kp ±0.1 V/A, 0.5–10) and > / < (Ti ±1 ms, 1–100 ms), applied live. Generated by `tests/l1/design/connection_apf.py --sweep docs/testing/design/D-GFL-10_bench`.

**Model**: D-GFL-09 as fitted to the bench ([report](../D-GFL-09_bench/report.md)): APF feedforward (unchanged), measured grid, 25 Ω behind the relay, 6 V of dead time, step 2b from the relay command. The gains apply from the start. 0.1 A Id reference step 0.6 s after the contact (the bench's step 3 size). Current PI output limit ±30 V. Trips: the grid observer (100 ms delay) and the 3 A current trip.

**Reading the tables**: each cell is one (Kp, Ti); "trip" means the setting tripped before the end of the run.

## Changing the gains online

**Computed before the library made the change bumpless** (0caa792): here ∫e was kept, so the integral term jumped by the ratio of the new to the old Kp/Ti at the change; the Kp 5 / Ti 5 ms trip comes from that jump. The model now carries the integral term over, as the firmware does; rerun the sweep to refresh this table. On the bench the gains change while connected (keys + − > <; the telemetry reports kp and ti; every capture and firmware dump records the gains in force). The model applies the same change: today's gains (Kp 3.5 V/A, Ti 20 ms) through the connection, the new gains 0.3 s after the contact, the 0.1 A step at 0.6 s. `run(gains=...)` takes such a schedule, and `bench_gain_schedule()` rebuilds it from a bench console (the key presses since the last boot).

## Limits

- Same as D-GFL-09: averaged bridge, fitted grid and dead time, no SYNC_LOST; the ff 1 trip was not reproduced there, so a setting that survives here is a candidate, not a guarantee.
- The one-period mean that removes the 2ω ripple also slows the measured rise time (floor about 10 ms).
- A result here never passes or fails the firmware (ADR 0004).
